Your NCLEX checklist pack

8-week checklist

  1. Week 1: book your exam window, take a baseline 75-question assessment, list your three weakest client-need categories.
  2. Weeks 2–3: Management of Care + Safety & Infection Control — 75 questions/day, read every rationale.
  3. Weeks 4–5: Pharmacology + Physiological Adaptation — drug classes, antidotes, lab values.
  4. Week 6: Reduction of Risk + Basic Care & Comfort; start NGN case studies.
  5. Week 7: two timed full-length practice exams; re-study missed areas only.
  6. Week 8: light mixed sets, sleep, logistics. Stop studying 24 hours before.

Test-day checklist

  • Authorization to Test (ATT) email saved
  • Acceptable, unexpired government ID — name matches your registration exactly
  • Arrive 30 minutes early; know the test-centre rules
  • Eat, hydrate, and plan your optional breaks

Weak-area tracker

After each practice set, log the category of every miss. Re-study only categories that stay above 30% wrong for two sets in a row.
