## Gaps NurseNow fills

1. **One place for US + Canada + IEN licensing.** Most competitors are US-only.
2. **Free, private tools** (pay, offers, shift planner with calendar export, drip calculator, licence tracker, study planner) — most competitors gate these behind accounts.
3. **Daily automated freshness** (jobs, news, videos) on a fast static site.
4. **Transparent monetisation**: labelled sponsorships, featured jobs, Academy waitlist, donations, contests.

## Monetisation stack implemented

AdSense (Auto ads + optional manual slots) · featured job listings & recruiter plans · career-match leads (opt-in employer sharing) · Academy waitlist ($19–$99 courses) · resume boost · affiliate gear guide · sponsorships/media kit · donations/pledges · contest sponsorships.
