How to stand out when you apply

Most hospital systems screen applications with an applicant tracking system (ATS) before a human sees them. Mirror the posting’s exact wording for your specialty, unit type and certifications, list your license type and jurisdiction near the top, and keep formatting simple. Our free resume builder suggests specialty keywords and prints a clean, ATS-friendly PDF.

Before you accept, compare the whole package — base rate, differentials, overtime rules, orientation length, ratios and float policy — not just the headline number. The pay calculator compares two offers side by side.

Jobs FAQ

Where do these jobs come from?

Every morning an automated job pulls fresh nursing openings from public job APIs and feeds, removes duplicates and expired listings, and rebuilds this page. Featured listings are posted directly by employers. Each “Apply” button takes you to the original posting.

Does NurseNow charge nurses anything?

No. Searching, applying and every tool on NurseNow are free for nurses. Employers pay for featured placement, which is always labelled.

How do I see more jobs near me?

Use the one-click search launcher above: type a role and a city and open the same search on Indeed, LinkedIn, Job Bank (Canada), USAJOBS, ZipRecruiter or Google Jobs.

How do I avoid job scams?

Never pay to apply, never share your SIN/SSN or banking details before a verified offer, and confirm recruiters through the employer’s official website. Report suspicious listings to us via the contact page.
