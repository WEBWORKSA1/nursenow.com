NCLEX FAQ

How many questions are on the NCLEX?

Both the NCLEX-RN and NCLEX-PN use computerized adaptive testing with a minimum of 85 and a maximum of 150 items, including unscored pretest items, within a five-hour limit. The exam stops when it is 95% confident you are above or below the passing standard.

How do I register?

Apply for licensure with your nursing regulatory body first, then register and pay with Pearson VUE. You can schedule once you receive your Authorization to Test (ATT).

What is the Next Generation NCLEX?

Since April 2023 the NCLEX includes clinical-judgment case studies and new item types — such as bow-tie, matrix, drag-and-drop and highlight — built on NCSBN’s Clinical Judgment Measurement Model, with partial-credit scoring on many items.

How long should I study?

Most candidates plan 6–10 weeks of structured study. Daily practice questions with careful rationale review matter more than total hours.
