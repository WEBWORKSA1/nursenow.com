Gear guide

Gear that earns its place in your pocket

What to look for, what to skip, and why — organised by what you’ll use every shift.

Disclosure: some links are affiliate links — NurseNow may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes what we recommend. Learn more.

Stethoscope

You’ll use it every shift for heart, lung and bowel sounds. A good acoustic model outlasts several cheap ones.

  • Dual-head or tunable diaphragm
  • Comfortable, well-sealed ear tips
  • Tubing long enough to reach without bending over
  • Specialty units may supply single-patient scopes — don’t overspend before you know your unit

Supportive work shoes

Twelve hours on hard floors is where back, knee and foot pain starts. Shoes are the highest-return purchase.

  • Slip-resistant outsole
  • Fluid-resistant, wipeable upper
  • Firm heel counter and arch support
  • Rotate two pairs so each dries out
Shop nursing shoes ↗

Graduated compression socks

Light graduated compression can reduce leg fatigue and swelling on long shifts.

  • Graduated compression (15–20 mmHg is common for work)
  • Moisture-wicking fabric
  • Correct size by calf measurement
  • Ask your clinician first if you have circulation problems
Shop compression socks ↗

Scrubs

Comfortable, durable scrubs make long shifts easier — and many units require specific colours.

  • Four-way stretch and moisture wicking
  • Enough secure pockets for pens, tape and phone
  • Check your employer’s colour policy
  • Wash-tested durability
Shop scrubs ↗

Penlight

Pupil checks, mouth and throat inspection and low-light assessment.

  • Warm or neutral LED
  • Pupil gauge printed on the barrel
  • Reusable and easy to clean
Shop penlights ↗

Trauma shears

For dressings, tape and clothing removal in emergencies.

  • Blunt safety tip
  • Autoclavable or easy to disinfect
  • Comfortable grip
Shop trauma shears ↗

Watch with a second hand

Counting respirations and pulses, and timing reassessments and drips.

  • Clear second hand or seconds display
  • Wipeable silicone band
  • Clip or fob styles for infection-control policies that limit wrist jewellery
Shop nurse watches ↗

Clipboard & report sheet organiser

Keeps your brain sheet, tape and pens together through a hectic shift.

  • Folding or storage design that fits in a pocket or bag
  • Privacy cover for patient information
  • Easy to wipe down

Insulated lunch bag & water bottle

Real food and steady hydration help energy and decision-making on 12s and nights.

  • Holds two meals and snacks
  • Leak-proof bottle you’ll actually refill
  • Fits your unit locker
