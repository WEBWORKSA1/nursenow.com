FAQ

Who sees my job?

Nurses actively searching NurseNow’s jobs page, homepage, weekly brief and relevant guides. Featured roles are pinned to the top until they expire.

How fast does it go live?

Usually within one business day after we confirm details and payment.

Can you invoice us?

Yes. Choose “Invoice me” in the form and we’ll send an invoice to your billing contact.

Do you offer refunds?

If your listing hasn’t gone live, we refund in full. Once live, we’ll extend or edit it instead.
