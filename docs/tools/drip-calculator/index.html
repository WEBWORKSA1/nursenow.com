Formulas used

  • Drops per minute = volume (mL) × drop factor (gtt/mL) ÷ time (minutes)
  • mL per hour = volume (mL) ÷ time (hours)
  • Weight-based rate (mL/hr) = dose (mcg/kg/min) × weight (kg) × 60 ÷ concentration (mcg/mL)
  • Desired over have = desired dose ÷ dose on hand × quantity

Safety: this calculator is a study and double-check aid only. It does not replace your facility’s smart-pump drug library, independent double checks for high-alert medications, or prescriber orders. See our medical disclaimer.
