    Building a safer schedule

    Research on shift work consistently links long runs of consecutive 12-hour shifts and quick returns (going from a night straight into a day) with fatigue and error risk. Cluster nights together, give yourself at least one full recovery day after a night run, and avoid flipping back to days on the same day you finish nights. Our night shift survival guide covers sleep, light and caffeine timing.
