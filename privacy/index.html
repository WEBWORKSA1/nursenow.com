Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

This policy explains how NurseNow (“we”), operated by Webworks, handles personal information on nursenow.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit through forms — such as your name, email, phone, goals, job details or messages. We use it only to respond, deliver what you requested (plans, newsletters, listings) and, where you opted in, to share your profile with vetted employers.
  • Tool data — inputs in our tools are stored in your browser’s local storage and are not sent to us.
  • Usage data — standard server logs and, where enabled, aggregate analytics.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our private inbox through a third-party form-relay service (FormSubmit). That provider processes the data only to transmit it.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), a consent message is shown before personalised ads are served.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under Canada’s PIPEDA and Québec’s Law 25, the GDPR, or US state privacy laws) you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information. Use the contact form and select “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity.

Retention and security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you gave them and apply reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

Children

NurseNow is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
