Your licence doesn’t stay current on its own. Renewal rules differ by jurisdiction, and the terminology is confusing. Here’s what CE hours, CEUs and quality-assurance programs mean — and how to stay compliant without last-minute panic.

[[toc]]

## Contact hours vs CEUs

- A **contact hour** is typically 50–60 minutes of approved learning.
- A **continuing education unit (CEU)** usually equals **10 contact hours**.

Many boards express requirements in contact hours, so check which unit your board uses before you count.

## United States: requirements vary by state

Each state board of nursing sets its own renewal rules. Common features include:

- a set number of contact hours per renewal period (often every two years);
- **mandatory topics** in some states — for example, implicit bias, human trafficking, domestic violence, opioid prescribing (for APRNs) or infection control;
- alternatives such as national certification or academic credit in some states; and
- a small number of states that don’t require CE for RN renewal at all.

Several states use **CE Broker** to track compliance, and many accredited providers report completions automatically. Always confirm current rules on your board’s website, especially if you hold multiple licences.

## Canada: quality assurance and continuing competence

Canadian regulators typically require **annual renewal** plus participation in a quality-assurance or continuing-competence program rather than a fixed number of CE hours. In Ontario, for example, the College of Nurses of Ontario’s Quality Assurance Program includes a self-assessment and a learning plan, and nurses may be selected for further assessment. Other provinces have similar programs with practice-hour requirements. Keep your learning plan and evidence of activities in case you’re selected.

## Choosing accredited CE

- Look for providers accredited by the **ANCC** or approved by your state board.
- Prioritise courses that match your practice and goals — they count for renewal *and* make you better at your job.
- Free CE is widely available from reputable providers; paid unlimited subscriptions can be cost-effective if you need many hours.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many CE hours do nurses need?
It depends on your state or province. Many US states require a set number of contact hours every renewal period; Canadian regulators typically use annual quality-assurance programs instead. Check your regulator’s website.

### Does certification count toward CE?
Some states accept a current national certification toward renewal requirements, and certification renewal itself often requires CE. Confirm with your board.

### Do CPR or BLS courses count as CE?
Many boards don’t accept basic life-support recertification as CE. Check your board’s rules before counting it.
